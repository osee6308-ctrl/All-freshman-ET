Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how All Freshman ET collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information — Name, phone number, university, stream, and hashed password.
  • App Usage Data — Practice exam scores and progress.
  • Device Info — Browser type, OS, and a unique Device ID (used for account locking and security).

2. Where Data Is Stored

Your account and progress data are securely stored in our cloud database (Cloudflare D1). Some data (like session tokens and downloaded study materials) are cached locally on your device for faster performance and offline access.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and secure your account
  • To track your exam progress and scores
  • To prevent account sharing (one account per device)
  • To send Admin login OTP codes via email

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with infrastructure providers necessary to run the app (e.g., Cloudflare for hosting and databases).

5. Content Rules

You agree not to abuse the platform, share your account, or attempt to bypass security measures.

6. Children's Privacy

All Freshman ET is not intended for children under 13.

7. Your Rights

  • Delete your local content anytime by clearing app storage.
  • Contact admin to request deletion of your cloud account and data.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gada collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Content you post — text, images, embedded videos
  • Interactions — likes, comments, tips, shares
  • Messages — chats between users
  • Device info — browser type, OS, screen size

2. Where Data Is Stored

All content you create in Gada is stored locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage). Gada does not upload your posts, images, or personal data to any external server.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your posts and interactions in the app
  • To enable chat and notifications
  • To improve app features and fix bugs

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, except:

  • When required by law
  • To protect user safety
  • With your explicit consent

5. Content Rules

You agree not to post nudity, violence, harassment, spam, or illegal content. Violations may result in a permanent ban.

6. Children's Privacy

Gada is not intended for children under 13.

7. Your Rights

  • Delete your content anytime by clearing storage
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data

8. Cookies and Tracking

Gada does not use cookies or third-party tracking.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted here with a new date.
